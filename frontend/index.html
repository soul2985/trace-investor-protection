About TRACE

What is TRACE?
TRACE is an independent investor-safety verification tool created to protect ordinary citizens against financial scams and fraudulent investment claims.

SEBI Snapshot Verification:
TRACE matches claimed registration numbers against an authentic database of 3,321 verified Investment Advisers and Research Analysts as of October 03, 2026.

Emergency Contact:
If you have been scammed or transferred money, immediately dial 1930 (National Cybercrime Reporting Portal) or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
